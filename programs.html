Program 02 · The real process

Operations and process

The documented process and the actual one differ in almost every organisation, and it is the actual one that produces the delays. We reconstruct it with the people who do the work, and we look first for steps to remove. Putting a system on top of a process nobody questioned makes the problem permanent and more expensive.

Program 03 · Scope drift tracked

Programme delivery

Programmes rarely fail in one dramatic moment; they fail through small scope changes nobody wrote down. We hold the scope in writing and report every change with its effect on date and cost, including when that contradicts what has been said internally. That is uncomfortable occasionally and it is the job.

Program 04 · And what we refer

Advisory support

Ongoing work is where an unregulated adviser drifts into regulated territory, usually helpfully. Investments and pensions need FCA authorisation. Reserved legal activities need a regulated lawyer. Accounts, tax and audit need the relevant professional. We refer rather than offering a view in passing, and we disclose anything that could affect our objectivity.
